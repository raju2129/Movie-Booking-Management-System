FAQs

How do I cancel a booking?
Go to My Bookings in your dashboard. Select the booking and click Cancel. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
Can I reschedule tickets?
Rescheduling is not directly supported. Please cancel and rebook. Cancellations are free up to 2 hours before the show.
How do I get my confirmation?
Confirmation is shown immediately on-screen after booking. You can also view it in My Bookings at any time.
Are there convenience fees?
A small convenience fee is added per ticket at checkout. The exact amount is shown before you confirm payment.
